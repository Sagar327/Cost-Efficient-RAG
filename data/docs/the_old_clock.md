# The Old Clock

Maya lived in a small town surrounded by green hills and quiet forests. She had always been curious about the old clock tower that stood in the center of the town. The clock had stopped working many years ago, but nobody seemed to know exactly why.

One rainy Saturday morning, Maya decided to visit the tower. She carried a small flashlight and an old notebook that belonged to her grandfather. The tower door was unlocked, so she carefully stepped inside.

A narrow wooden staircase led to the top. As Maya climbed, she noticed several old photographs hanging on the walls. One photograph showed the town square nearly a hundred years ago. In the center of the picture stood the same clock tower, surrounded by people celebrating a festival.

When Maya reached the clock mechanism, she discovered that one of the gears was covered with dust. She also found a small brass key lying beneath the mechanism. Curious, she wrote everything down in her notebook.

The key had a tiny number engraved on it: 1924.

Maya returned home and showed the key to her grandfather. He smiled when he saw it. He explained that 1924 was the year the clock tower was built. According to an old family story, the clock had stopped during a powerful storm decades ago.

The next morning, Maya returned to the tower with her grandfather. Together, they cleaned the mechanism and carefully examined the gears. Her grandfather discovered that a small piece of wood had become stuck between two gears.

After removing the wood, Maya turned the brass key.

At first, nothing happened.

Then the largest gear slowly began to move.

A deep sound echoed through the tower as the clock hands started turning again. When the clock reached twelve, its bell rang across the town for the first time in many years.

People came out of their houses and gathered in the square. Maya looked at her grandfather, who was smiling proudly.

From that day forward, the old clock became a symbol of the town's history. Maya kept the brass key safely in her notebook, and whenever she passed the tower, she remembered the rainy morning when she discovered its secret.
